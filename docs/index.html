Core Capability Specs

100% Privacy Focused

No account required, no online trackers, no advertisements, and zero background analytics. All logging databases are saved entirely locally on your SQLite device storage.

Reactor V2 Ledger

Aggregates 1-minute vehicle heartbeats (RPM, voltage, thermal load) into a high-performance local ledger. Detects outliers, tracks diagnostics logs, and syncs sensors seamlessly.

Local SQL Agentic Chat

Query your ledger in natural language. The Gemini API translates questions (e.g. "When was my engine under maximum strain?") into SQL, queries the database, and narrates insights.

Hardware Calibrations

Built-in configurations for hardware quirks, including nominal fuel multiplier scalars to correct tank size differences (e.g. Renault/Nissan CMF-A+ generic 70L scaling to physical 40L tanks).

Canvas Trend Visualizer

Custom Jetpack Compose Canvas graphs with clear, scaling relative x-axis duration timestamps and y-axis metric bounds, ensuring clean data representations with zero layout overlap.

Android Auto HUD

Integrates natively with Android Auto templates to mirror active speeds, RPM levels, and thermal conditions safely onto your vehicle's primary center console display.
